// about

A builder who automates the tedious parts.

I'm a software engineer based in Nairobi with a large, active project history: dozens of repositories spanning web automation and scrapers, data-extraction and reporting pipelines, file and workflow automation, and increasingly LLM-driven assistants and agents. I build full-stack products end to end, from API and data layer through to UI and deployment.

That builder's instinct is grounded in day-to-day engineering at a bank, where I integrate digital channels with core systems and payment rails. The through-line across all of it: take a messy, manual, or brittle process and turn it into something automated, typed, and reliable. I hold certifications in cloud and network security and ethical hacking, and that security-first habit follows me into everything I ship.
